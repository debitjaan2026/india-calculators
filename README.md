## How it works

Each file in this repo is the exact snippet that runs on the blog page above:

- `emi-calculator.html` — monthly EMI from the standard formula E = P × r × (1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1), plus total interest and total payment.
- `sip-calculator.html` — monthly SIP growth with compounding. Every figure is labelled an estimate; mutual fund returns are not guaranteed.
- `fd-calculator.html` — fixed-deposit maturity value with quarterly compounding, the Indian bank standard, plus interest earned.
- `gst-calculator.html` — add or remove GST at 5%, 12%, 18% or 28%, with the CGST/SGST split.
- `age-calculator.html` — exact age in years, months and days, including age on any exam or job cut-off date, plus total days lived and next birthday.

## Verified figures

- EMI: ₹1,00,000 loan, 10% interest, 1 year → ₹8,792 per month.
- SIP: ₹5,000 per month for 10 years → ₹6,00,000 invested, estimated total ₹11,61,695.
- FD: ₹1,00,000 at 6.5% for 5 years → maturity ₹1,38,042.
- GST: ₹1,000 + 18% → ₹1,180; removing 18% from ₹1,180 returns ₹1,000.
- Age: date of birth plus a cut-off date gives the exact age on that date.
